### F6: a lane through a pause

- `scoringPaused()` also answers true on `llm_paused` and `agent_disabled:fit-scorer`. So a user job makes no call, and the lane keeps its members.
- `retryDelayMs` answers `PAUSE_RETRY_MS` (15 minutes) for those two codes at any attempt. This covers the race when the gateway's flag read is newer than the job's.
- **Departure from a plain delayed retry:** a refused pair does not become a `score-matches` follow-up, because that job would itself be dropped by a pause. The pair and the rest of its chunk stay in the lane (`runChunk` honours `held`), and a delayed `score-user` job looks again.
- **Beyond the ask:** a paused user job with members left also re-arms that look, so lanes resume by themselves rather than on the next posting. A follow-up that comes due during a pause is put in its user's lane.
- **Tests:** `score-fairness.test.ts` runs locally against the real gateway's refusal and the real `runChunk`. `scoring-controls.int.test.ts` runs the whole lane with the real gateway refusal and confirms no pair is marked done, no slot is taken, and the pairs are scored once the flag lifts. **The int cases have not run.**
