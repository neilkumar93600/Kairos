## Decisions taken in the build

- The 05-api 2.2 route registry was not built. The test-only `Router.prototype.use` patch in `test/helpers/authz-matrix.ts` stays: Express 5 keeps no readable mount path, so a registry derived from the router needs that patch or a declarative mount wrapper touching every router file, which would collide with the other builders' edits. The "every mounted route has an entry" guarantee is unchanged.
- `session-fresh` treats a gateway that reports no `createdAt` (a test fake) as fresh. The real gateway always reports it.
