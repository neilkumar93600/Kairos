# WP-47 frontend handoff

No route changes shape. `POST /api/billing/checkout` still answers `{ url }`, and the 409 `already_subscribed` and 503 `billing_unavailable` answers from WP-10 are unchanged. Nothing in `web/` was edited by this package.

When the owner turns on `STRIPE_AUTOMATIC_TAX` (owner-actions/WP-47.md step 7), Stripe's Checkout page asks for a billing address and an optional tax id, and adds tax on top of the plan price. The price a customer sees on our pricing page is then no longer the total for everyone. Release this copy together with that flip, not before: while the flag is off, no tax is added.

## 1. Pricing copy: prices exclude tax (release with the flip)

Under the plan grid on the pricing page and on the billing page's plan picker, add one line:

> Prices in US dollars. Sales tax, VAT or GST is added at checkout where it applies.

Where the per-plan price is shown with "/month", "/week" or "/quarter", leave the number as it is. The prices in `shared/src/plans.ts` stay tax-exclusive.

## 2. Nothing else is required

- The billing address, tax id and tax line all live on Stripe's hosted Checkout page and in Stripe's receipts. The web collects none of them.
- Customers edit their billing address and tax id in the Stripe portal (the existing "Change, pause or cancel" button). No new UI is needed.
- A misconfigured flip (the flag on before Stripe Tax is activated) shows as the existing `503 billing_unavailable` message on checkout.
