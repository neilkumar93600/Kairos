# Kairos Landing, Light Rebuild Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Rebuild `/` in the owner-approved light style (Duna primary) on the reference structure (Nivara/Liftoff), at reference density, so the owner can preview it on `localhost:3000`.

**Architecture:** Same Next.js 16 route group `(landing)` and shell from Drop 1. Visual system, section markup and copy are replaced; the logic that survives (approve store, fit-run, calculator, format, odometer, motion preference) is kept. The app outside the landing adopts the same light palette through `globals.css`.

**Tech Stack:** Next.js 16.4.0, React 19.3.0, Tailwind CSS 4, TypeScript 5.9, `next/font` (Inter, Inter Tight, Instrument Serif), `next/image` with sharp. No new dependencies.

**Spec (read in this order; later files override earlier ones):** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-15-kairos-landing-design/07-reference-system.md` (archetypes A0 to A11, layout), `08-restructure-plan.md` (sections, exact copy, wireframes, reuse map in section 7, art briefs in section 3), `09-style-direction.md` (Duna tokens, type, colour roles, and section 9: the changes it makes to 08). Owner decisions (2026-10-15, all defaults): Duna style; app follows the light palette; hero pill "25 free applications"; paintings generated by an image model; pricing shows plan cards only (calculator stays on `/pricing`); logo in ink, lime only as the 8 px status dot.

## Global Constraints

- Density: per section at most a 1 to 3 word pill, a headline of at most 8 words, one subline of at most 20 words, at most 3 short items, one visual, one CTA. Copy comes from 08 section 1 verbatim (with 09 section 9 changes).
- Token roles from 09 are binding: Espresso `#160f0c` fills buttons only; Aubergine Ink `#1b0624` for headings and the one dark card; Graphite `#444444` body; muted `#5e5566`; art hues only inside art layers; lime `#7fee64` only as an 8 px status dot with a 1 px ink ring beside its word.
- Instrument Serif 400 only on the four art lines (hero H1, spotlight H2, dark card H2, closing line), never below 36 px. Weights 400 and 500 only.
- Truth: no logos, testimonials, counts or outcome claims; every product visual is a real DOM card with a SAMPLE chip and synthetic names.
- Next.js 16 differs from older versions: read `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before Next-specific code.
- Readable without JavaScript; every motion has a reduced-motion fallback gated on `html[data-motion="reduced"]` and the media query; WCAG 2.2 AA contrast; no horizontal scroll at 360 px.
- Landing CSS scoped under `.lp`. Edit only the files your task owns; report other needs as requests.

## Review Focus

1. Hero painting fails to load or loads late: headline and CTA readable on the canvas colour; no layout shift (fixed aspect box).
2. Text over painted art: contrast still AA at every breakpoint crop.
3. JavaScript disabled: every section visible; demos show their static state.
4. Reduced motion: no float, parallax, drift or drawn connector animation.
5. Signed-in app pages and `/pricing` after the palette change: readable, no dark leftovers.
