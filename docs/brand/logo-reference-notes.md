# Kairos Logo Reference Notes

Recorded from owner feedback on 2026-10-15.

## Status

- The boxed `K`, custom-`K` monogram, wordmark-only explorations, and the first independent-symbol sheets were rejected.
- The next direction must use an original standalone symbol paired with a custom-feeling `Kairos` wordmark.
- The supplied Logoipsum SVGs are visual references only. Do not copy their paths or ship an unaltered placeholder as the Kairos identity.

## What the owner likes in the supplied examples

1. A mirrored four-part blue mark with a strong, energetic silhouette.
2. A bright green rounded tile containing a complex white organic path.
3. An orange asymmetrical rounded tile with a crisp negative-space crossing and a detached detail.
4. A bright green gestural loop paired with a dark-green italic wordmark.
5. A layered lime-and-deep-green badge with an inset negative-space focal point and an italic wordmark.

## Shared visual language

- Independent abstract logomark; never a letter or monogram.
- Compact, self-contained silhouette that reads at roughly 40px.
- Bold filled masses rather than thin outlines.
- Curved or aerodynamic geometry with visible energy and direction.
- Strong use of negative space inside the symbol.
- One bright accent paired with a much darker companion colour or white.
- Asymmetry is welcome when the overall shape remains balanced.
- A wordmark that feels drawn for the mark: slightly forward, compact, or otherwise customized rather than default UI type.
- Abstract meaning is preferred over literal job-search imagery.

## Kairos translation

The Kairos mark should feel like a decisive opening, a controlled release, or several forces aligning at the right moment. The meaning should remain abstract; it should not depict a job, resume, briefcase, letter `K`, clock, hourglass, check mark, door, or AI sparkle.

Use the existing product palette as the starting material rather than copying the reference colours:

- Aubergine Ink `#1b0624` for the wordmark and dark structural mass.
- One energetic accent drawn from the site's art palette, tested individually: Coral `#f08a5d`, Dusk `#6f74c9`, or Meadow `#a9b48c`.
- Linen `#f7f7f5` for negative-space cutouts and light reversal.

## Requirements for the next concept round

- Produce three original SVG-native mark-plus-wordmark systems.
- Each mark must be structurally different, not the same motif with altered curves.
- Show the mark alone at 40px, the horizontal lockup, and a 16px legibility proof.
- Use flat colour only: no gradients, shadows, texture, or raster effects.
- Check silhouette similarity against the supplied references and direct competitors before presenting.
- Do not change the live site, application icons, extension icons, or `prompt.md` until one direction is explicitly approved.

## Licensing boundary

Logoipsum describes its assets as placeholder logos. Its published license permits use in mockups and UI projects, but says an unaltered logo may not be used as a final logo, trademark, service mark, or brand identity. Kairos therefore needs new geometry even when a reference is licensed for placeholder use.
